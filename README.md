# ColourblindGame

A game that shows off how games can easily be difficult for the visually impaired to play.

## Basic game idea
This is a platforming game where different platforms and enemies/obstacles are colour-coded. The brown enemy might be bad, and the red enemy might be good. White platforms might be safe, and pink platforms might crumble beneath you.

First, the player (assumed to be a trichromat) walks through the first 'level' of the game where they get from one end of the level to the next. Then, once they've reached the end a colourblind filter is applied to the game, hopefully emulating what a colourblind person could potentially see. It will be made stronger purposely. The player then has to traverse back through the level, but this time with the visual impairment.

On the second 'level', the player will recognise and deal with the struggle of not being able to tell the difference between different colours. They will have to figure out how to traverse the level through trial and error rather than rules and logic.

Depending on the brief or whatever, I might make an option to have a toggle for colourblind accessibility features that make the second level easily passable via the use of unique textures and symbols and sounds and stuff, but idk yet.
